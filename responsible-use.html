Responsible Use & Risk

This publication is intended for adults aged 18+ where betting activity is lawful. Betting can cause financial loss and should not be treated as guaranteed income.

Access is not a financial signal

Being able to log in, open a Wallet dashboard or create an account does not make a wager safe or profitable.

Protect credentials

Do not reveal passwords, OTPs, payment PINs, banking passwords, recovery codes or remote-access codes to speed up an account, ID or Wallet task.

Stop conditions

Do not borrow money to bet or chase losses. If betting is causing harm to finances, work, study, relationships or wellbeing, stopping is a valid action and suitable local support may be appropriate.
